    Projects

      Studio

      Spnvisualz turns a song into a place you can step inside.

      The video, the loop, the cover and the cutdowns are built as one world, so a release looks the same everywhere it lands.

      Services

      • Music videos

        Performance, narrative and one-take pieces. Treatment, shoot, edit and colour grade, with a 16:9 master and vertical cutdowns.

      • Visualizers

        Spotify Canvas loops (3–8 s, 9:16), lyric and audio-reactive videos, and loops for stage screens.

      • Cover art & stills

        Single and album artwork at 3000 × 3000 px, press photos and stills pulled from the shoot.

      • Brand & social

        Reels, TikTok and Shorts at 1080 × 1920, teasers, recaps and motion titles.

      How a project runs

      1. BriefA short call about the track, the release plan and the budget.
      2. TreatmentA written concept with references, locations and a shot list. You approve it before anything is booked.
      3. ProductionThe shoot day, or build days for animated visuals.
      4. DeliveryEdit, colour grade and a review round, then the master and every cutdown you need.
