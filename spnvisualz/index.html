Quest log

From first call to final files

  1. Stage 1

    Brief

    A short call about the track, the release plan and the budget. Send a link to the song if you have it.

  2. Stage 2

    Treatment

    A written concept with references, locations and a shot list. You approve it before anything is booked.

  3. Stage 3

    Production

    The shoot day, or build days for animated visuals and loops.

  4. Stage 4

    Delivery

    Edit, colour grade and a review round, then the master and every cutdown you need.
